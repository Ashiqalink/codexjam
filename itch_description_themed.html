Shipwrecked. Broke. Running for president.

You wash up on Isla Corrupta with one flip-flop and no plan. Five officials run the island. Every one of them has something to hide.

COUP D'ETAT, BABY! is a low-poly stealth-brawler about blackmail, baguettes and one very dirty election.

THE JOB
  • Scavenge. Loot wrecks, safes and desks for cash.
  • Sneak. Slip past guards, hide, climb, or knock them out.
  • Shop. Spend your winnings on ever-sillier weapons at the market.
  • Snoop. Break into government buildings and hunt for secret dossiers.
  • Squeeze. Make each official an offer they can't refuse.
